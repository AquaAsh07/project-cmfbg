# LEGACY — The Career Football Game

> **No cards. No coins. No live service. Just your career.**

A AAA-quality, offline-first football game where **career mode is the game** — not a side mode. Player Career, Manager Career, and Club Career (Road to Glory) in one persistent world. Fully playable 3D matches. Premium on Steam. No microtransactions, ever.

**Project codename:** CMFBG  
**Engine:** Unity 6 LTS  
**Platform:** PC (Steam) → Console  
**Status:** Design Lock — GDD v1.0 complete, ready for Vertical Slice

---

## The Vision

We grew up on FIFA and PES career modes — creating a player at 16, earning trust, winning the league, becoming a legend. Modern football games abandoned that for live-service monetization. LEGACY brings it back, rebuilt with modern tech, for Steam.

- ✅ **All careers:** Player + Manager + Club in one world
- ✅ **Playable 3D:** You play matches, not just menus
- ✅ **Offline forever:** Your save lives on your PC, not a server
- ✅ **Earn everything:** No loot boxes, no pay-to-win, no coins
- ✅ **AAA feel:** Broadcast, commentary, crowd, motion capture

## Why Steam First?

Largest indie football audience, Workshop mods, cloud saves, no cert costs, fastest path to players. Console (PS5/Xbox) after PC proves the game.

## Why Fictional World?

Real licenses cost millions/year. Our fictional world (8 nations, 120+ clubs) means 100% ownership, moddable, no yearly reskin. Rename clubs via editor if you want.

## Why Unity 6?

Fastest to Steam for a small team, C# ecosystem, DOTS crowd performance, Asset Store sports assets. Unreal is heavier; Godot's sports ecosystem is smaller.
