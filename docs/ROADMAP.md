## Phase 0: Design (Month 1) — NOW
- [x] GDD v1.0 — vision, pillars, modes, systems
- [ ] Tech prototype: Unity 6 setup, ball physics, player controller
- [ ] Art test: 1 stadium, 1 player, broadcast camera
- [ ] Team roles: Lead Dev, Gameplay, UI, Art, Audio

**Exit:** GDD approved, Unity project running, ball rolls and bounces.

---

## Phase 1: Vertical Slice (Months 2–4)
**Question to answer:** *Is the football fun?*

- Player creation (name, position, appearance)
- 1 club, 10-team league, fixtures + table
- Playable 3D match: move, pass, shoot, tackle, score, celebrate
- Career hub: advance week, match ratings, save/load (1 slot)
- 1 stadium, basic crowd, broadcast camera

**Exit Criteria:**
- Playable for 30 minutes, fun
- 60fps on GTX 1060 / Steam Deck
- Save persists after restart

---

## Phase 2: Player Career Alpha (Months 5–8)
- Attributes (0–99), growth, training, trust, contracts
- Transfers, loans, injuries, lifestyle, press
- 3 leagues, 40 clubs, full season sim
- Commentary (basic), 5 stadiums, weather
- Difficulty levels + custom sliders

**Exit:** Play age 16 → 17 season, earn transfer, injuries work.

---

## Phase 3: Manager & Club Career (Months 9–12)
- Manager: tactics, formations, transfers, board, press, youth academy
- Club: finances, stadium upgrades, facilities, sponsorships
- Unified world: Player → Manager transition, Hall of Fame
- 6 leagues, 120 clubs, continental cup
- AI managers, transfer AI, world sim

**Exit:** All 3 modes playable, world stable for 5 seasons.

---

## Phase 4: Polish & Steam (Months 13–15)
- Full commentary (5,000+ lines), motion capture, 30 stadiums
- Steamworks: achievements, cloud saves, Workshop
- Localization: EN, ES, PT, DE, FR
- Closed beta (500 players), balance pass
- Steam Deck Verified

**Exit:** 90%+ positive beta, no game-breaking bugs.

---

## Phase 5: Launch (Month 16)
- Steam 1.0 launch — $39.99
- Day-1 patch, community support
- DLC roadmap: New Nations, Legends, Stories

---

## Weekly Rhythm (From Phase 1 Onward)

- **Friday:** Bug fix, polish, Steam devlog
